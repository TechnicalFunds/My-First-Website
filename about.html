About My Interests

A place to share what fascinates me about marine biology and entomology.

My introduction

I really care about the natural world. Insects and marine wildlife are some of my favorite areas of study.

What interests me

Learning about insects and marine animals interests me a lot. I like seeing how we depend on them and finding ways to help protect them.

Why insects and marine animals interest me

  • Seals are my favorite marine mammals.
  • The Caspian seal is one of my favorite species. Sadly, it is endangered, and I'd like to do what I can to help protect it.
  • I really love spiders, and I own a tarantula.
  • I used to have a jumping spider named Robby. Sadly, he passed away.
  • Marine animals and insects play important roles in ecosystems. Without them, ecosystems could be harmed.

A subject I find fascinating

Jumping spiders

Jumping spiders are fascinating to me. Some research suggests that they show sleep behaviors similar to dreaming. They may also be able to recognize human faces.
